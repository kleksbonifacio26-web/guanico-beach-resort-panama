Our story

More than a destination. A different way to live.

Guánico began in 2014 as a small surf camp welcoming travelers drawn to Panama's Pacific coast. Under new ownership since April 2024, it has evolved into an intimate, family-friendly beachfront resort.

Today, we welcome surfers, families, couples and adventure seekers who prefer authentic experiences to crowded tourist destinations.

Discover local traditions, friendly communities and the beauty of the Azuero Peninsula. Spend your days exploring, sharing great meals and enjoying the ocean without the rush of everyday life.

Good to know

Before you dine.

Where is Palmeras Restaurant located?

Palmeras Restaurant is located at Guánico Beach Resort in Playa Guánico Abajo, Tonosí, Los Santos, on Panama's Azuero Peninsula.

Can I visit without staying at the resort?

Visitors can inquire about dining without staying at the resort. Contact our team to confirm opening hours, table availability and private events before visiting.

Is breakfast included for hotel guests?

Yes. Breakfast is included with resort accommodation. Additional orders and other meals are charged separately.

Do you accommodate dietary requirements?

Please contact our restaurant before visiting to discuss allergies, vegetarian options and other dietary requirements.

How can I reserve a table?

Send us a WhatsApp message at +507 6772-5404 with your preferred date, time and number of guests. Our team will confirm availability.

Can you organize private dining and events?

Yes. Ask about birthdays, group meals, private gatherings and retreats, subject to availability and group requirements.

Is The Break part of Palmeras Restaurant?

The Break is presented as a separate local café option with its own menu, prices and operating arrangements.

Are the prices on this page current?

The displayed prices come from the published Palmeras sample menu and the supplied The Break menu. Prices may change. Confirm current prices with each venue.
