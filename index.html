06 / Our story

More than a destination. A different way to live.

Guánico began in 2014 as a small surf camp welcoming travelers drawn to the waves and natural beauty of Panama's Pacific coast. Under new ownership since April 2024, it has evolved into an intimate, family-friendly beachfront resort.

Today, we welcome surfers, families, couples and adventure seekers who prefer authentic experiences to crowded tourist destinations.

Discover local traditions, friendly communities and the beauty of the Azuero Peninsula. Spend your days exploring, sharing great meals and enjoying the ocean without the rush of everyday life.

Good to know

Before you arrive.

Where is Guánico Beach Resort located?

We are in Playa Guánico Abajo, Tonosí District, Los Santos Province, on Panama's Azuero Peninsula.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. Breakfast is included with accommodation. Room rates are quoted in USD, with an additional 10% hotel tax.

Are surfboards free for hotel guests?

Yes. Our complimentary demo program allows resort guests to try available surfboards and SUPs during their stay. Guided lessons and activities may cost extra.

Can beginners learn to surf here?

Yes. Coaching can be arranged for different skill levels. Our team can recommend suitable boards and sessions based on current ocean conditions.

Is the resort suitable for families?

Guánico is a family-friendly resort offering accommodation, dining, pool access and activities for different interests. Children should be supervised near the ocean and pool.

Can I reserve the entire resort?

Private resort stays may be arranged for families, celebrations, retreats and corporate groups, subject to availability and agreed terms.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

The published standard check-in time is 3 PM and check-out is noon. Contact reception to confirm your reservation details or request alternative arrangements.

Do you have a restaurant?

Yes. Palmeras Restaurant and our open-air bar and lounge offer coastal-inspired dining, international dishes, wood-fired pizza and refreshing drinks.

Are all activities included in the room rate?

No. Complimentary guest board demos are included, but guided tours, boat charters, coaching, horseback riding and other arranged activities may require additional payment.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please review the cancellation and deposit terms applicable to your chosen dates before confirming a reservation. Our reservations team can help clarify the terms.
