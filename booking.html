Our story

More than a destination. A different way to live.

Guánico began in 2014 as a small surf camp welcoming travelers drawn to the waves and natural beauty of Panama's Pacific coast. Under new ownership since April 2024, it has evolved into an intimate, family-friendly beachfront resort.

Today, we welcome surfers, families, couples and adventure seekers who prefer authentic experiences to crowded tourist destinations.

Discover local traditions, friendly communities and the beauty of the Azuero Peninsula. Spend your days exploring, sharing great meals and enjoying the ocean without the rush of everyday life.

Good to know

Before you arrive.

How do I book a room?

Select your dates, accommodation and guest details using the inquiry form. Send your request through WhatsApp so our reservations team can confirm availability, pricing and the next booking steps.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. Breakfast is included with accommodation. Room rates are in USD, with an additional 10% hotel tax.

Are surfboard demos free?

Yes. Complimentary equipment demos are available to resort guests, subject to board availability. Guided sessions and professional instruction may incur additional charges.

Can I reserve a board before arriving?

Yes. Choose Demo Surf & Stay in the inquiry form and provide your preferred model, experience level and session date. Our team will confirm availability.

Is the resort suitable for families?

Guánico welcomes families, couples, friends and solo travelers. Ask about suitable room configurations and age-appropriate activities. Children must be supervised around the pool and ocean.

Can we reserve the entire resort?

Private group stays can be arranged subject to availability and agreed terms. Contact us for a personalized accommodation, dining and activity proposal.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Standard check-in is 3 PM and check-out is noon. Alternative arrangements depend on availability and may involve additional charges.

Are there discounts for longer stays?

Longer-stay and group discounts may be available. Send your preferred dates and group details to request a personalized quote.

How do I get to Guánico Beach Resort?

We are located in Playa Guánico Abajo, Tonosí, Los Santos, Panama. Contact our team about road conditions, private transfers and transportation options.

What are the payment and cancellation terms?

Your applicable deposit, payment, cancellation and refund terms will be confirmed before booking. Please review the agreed conditions before making a payment.

Does submitting the form confirm my reservation?

No. The temporary WhatsApp form prepares an inquiry message for our reservations team. Your booking is confirmed only after the resort or its official booking engine confirms it.
